Xstream 0.9.8

Watch together, all new:
• Move friends' bubbles anywhere and pinch them bigger or smaller; they stay where you put them.
• Frame yourself: double-tap your own bubble and everyone sees you the way you chose.
• Mute one friend just for you, and see your own bubble too.
• Chat with emojis and favourites, and a small reaction button you can drag anywhere.
• Raise your hand to ask everyone to pause.
• See who joined and when everyone is back in sync.
• Start a watch party right from a title's page and invite friends at once.
• Calls use less data on mobile data or when the film is loading.

Xstream 0.9.5

Big screens get the TV look:
• Tablets turned sideways, laptops and PC players such as MuMu now show the TV-style Home, the side menu and the TV settings.
• Use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc like a TV remote, or the mouse and touch, all at the same time.
• Turn a tablet back upright and the phone layout returns; a video that is playing keeps playing.
• Laptops and PC players with memory to spare keep more of the video ready, for smoother HD and 4K.

Xstream 0.9.4

A calmer Home, HDR when you want it:
• Home stays still: rows and the Top 10 no longer change in front of you. Anything new shows the next time you open Home.
• Posters are ready as Home opens; while one loads, its card shows the poster's own colour instead of grey.
• Videos are prepared ahead in the cloud, so more titles are ready to play the moment you open them.
• Lighter on your phone: the heavy search sites only run when nothing else was found.
• HDR: turn it on in Quality and the picture moves to an HDR copy at the same moment, without stopping. Turn it off to go back. Your choice is remembered (only on screens that can show HDR).
