Xstream 0.2 beta

• Long-press any poster: it lifts out, the trailer plays inside the card, with ratings and Details · Play manually · Add to watchlist.
• The dock hides with a bubble while you scroll and pops back, with a red glow edge.
• Long-press My Space in the dock and slide to another profile; let go to switch.
• Library has four tabs: Watchlist, Calendar, Notifications and Watch together.
• Watchlist rooms: your own room plus one for each friend. Recommend titles to each other and say what you thought.
• Calendar: search to add any title, and pick the date and time in the app.
• Continue Watching › See all, with a button to clear it.
• My Space charts: your week (tap a day), movies vs series, and when you watch.
• Every episode remembers where you stopped.
• Trailers play directly, smoother and faster.
• New voice search: say a title, or ask for something and the AI answers.
• Player: play / pause at the bottom right next to ›; long titles never cover the clock; subtitles inside the video move up above the controls.
• Subtitle auto sync works quietly.
• Addons: rename pencil, long-press the name to copy the link, settings gear only where it applies.
• Quick Play is a simple on / off switch. Collections moved to Layout settings. GIFs play in the picker.

Xstream 0.1 beta

• Ticket Booth: pick your profile as a cinema ticket. The stub tears off and "Admit one" takes you in.
• After signing in, your profiles, pictures and settings come over from your account before "Who's watching?".
• Home has the card deck again, and the bottom dock has Home, Search, Library and My Space.
• Player buttons are plain icons and words, no glass.
• New logo colours: a red X with white signal waves.
• Watch together: friends, friend requests, introductions you approve, shared watchlists with plans, and chats.
• Library: your Watchlist with a calendar of new episodes, releases and your plans; Continue Watching; alerts.
• GIF profile pictures: search without a key, or paste any GIF link.
• Tips now finish for good when you tap Skip or Done.
